A few things you might wonder

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Is Smoke Less free?

Yes. The APK is free to download and use. There are no subscriptions or ads inside the app. The external MediaFire download page may show ads.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. Setup creates a profile on your phone, not an online account. Internet is needed to download the APK, but the Android app works offline afterward.

What happens if I uninstall the app?

Uninstalling or clearing app storage deletes your records and settings. Automatic cloud backups and device transfer are disabled. There is no recovery account or backup feature.

How do I update without losing my records?

Install a newer official APK over the existing app. It must have the same package and developer signing key. Don’t uninstall first. Earlier development builds are separate from the release app.

Why hasn’t my reminder appeared?

Check that reminders are enabled in Smoke Less and notifications are allowed in Android Settings. Battery-saving settings can delay delivery. Reminders use local scheduling rather than exact alarms.

Can I use it on iPhone or in a browser?

The downloadable app is for Android 7.0 and newer. This website is a showcase, not a browser version of the tracker. Its screenshots use sample data.
